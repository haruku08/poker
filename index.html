Hand rankings

What beats what?

Number 1 is the best. Any hand beats every hand below it. Only your best 5 cards count.

    Memory trick

    The harder a hand is to get, the more it's worth. That's why a flush (5 of one suit) beats a straight, and both lose to a full house.

    All suits are equal

    Hearts don't beat spades. If two hands are exactly the same, the pot is split.

    Texas Hold'em

    Play a hand, step by step

    You get 2 secret cards. 5 shared cards land in the middle. Make the best 5-card hand from those 7.

    Shared cards
    Your cards

    Good to know

    • You can use 2, 1 or even 0 of your own cards. Whatever makes the best 5.
    • If everyone else folds, you win straight away, and you don't have to show your cards.
    • The dealer button moves one seat to the left after every hand, so everyone gets a turn.
    • 2 to 10 players, one normal deck, no jokers.
    Chips & betting

    How much do I put in?

    All the chips in the middle are the pot. Whoever wins the hand takes it all.

    The blinds

    Before any cards are dealt, the two players left of the dealer must put in chips. That way there's always something to win.

    • Small blind: the player left of the dealer puts in a little (say 1 chip).
    • Big blind: the next player puts in double (2 chips).
    • The player after the big blind starts the betting. The blinds move round with the dealer.

    Your move

    Check

    Pass without betting. Only if nobody has bet yet.

    Bet

    Be the first to put chips in this round.

    Call

    Put in the same as the bet to stay in.

    Raise

    Match the bet and add more.

    Fold

    Give up this hand. Chips you put in stay in the pot.

    All-in

    Put in every chip you have.

    When is a betting round over?

    When everyone still playing has put in the same amount. Then the next cards come out.

    Let's watch a round

    Blinds are 1 and 2. Before the flop:

    Annasmall blind puts in 1
    Benbig blind puts in 2
    Chriscalls puts in 2
    Danaraises to 6
    Annafolds her 1 stays in the pot
    Bencalls adds 4 (now 6)
    Chriscalls adds 4 (now 6)

    Everyone left has 6 in. Pot: 1 + 6 + 6 + 6 = 19. Time for the flop!

    How big can a bet be?

    Fixed-limit best with kids

    Every bet is a set size: 2 chips in the first two rounds, 4 in the last two. Max 4 bets per round.

    Pot-limit

    You can bet up to the size of the pot.

    No-limit

    Bet anything, up to all your chips. The one you see on TV.

    Raising rules

    • The smallest bet is the big blind.
    • A raise must be at least as big as the last one. Someone bets 4? You raise to 8 or more.
    • Say "raise" first, then put in your chips all at once.

    Not enough chips?

    You're never out just because the bet is too big. Go all-in with what you have. You can only win as much from each player as you put in yourself. The extra chips from the others go into a side pot that only they can win.

    Example

    Sam goes all-in with 10. Lisa and Tom both put in 30. The main pot is 30 (10 from each) and anyone can win it. The side pot is 40, and only Lisa or Tom can win that.

    Starting chips at home

    10×white = 1
    8×red = 5
    5×blue = 10

    That's 100 each. Start with blinds 1 and 2. Want the game to finish before bedtime? Double the blinds every 20 to 30 minutes.

    Just two players?

    The dealer pays the small blind and goes first before the flop. After the flop, the other player goes first.

    Quick answers

    The questions that always start an argument.

    Does a full house beat a flush?

    YES
    beats

    Does a flush beat four of a kind ("poker")?

    NO

    Four of a kind wins. Only a straight flush or royal flush beats it.

    Does a flush beat a straight?

    YES

    5 cards of one suit is harder to get than 5 in a row.

    Does a straight beat three of a kind?

    YES

    Is a straight flush just a flush?

    NO

    It's in a row and one suit, which makes it the second best hand there is.

    Is A-2-3-4-5 a straight?

    YES

    It's the lowest straight. The Ace counts as 1 here.

    Is Q-K-A-2-3 a straight?

    NO

    No going around the corner. The Ace is either the top or the bottom.

    Is one suit better than another?

    NO

    All four suits are equal.

    Same pair? Does the next card count?

    YES

    The highest other card (the "kicker") wins.

    beats

    Two full houses: does the pair decide?

    NO

    The three-of-a-kind part decides first. Three 9s beat three 8s, whatever the pair.

    Are 3 pairs better than 2 pairs?

    NO

    Only 5 cards count, so you just use your two best pairs.

    Can it be a tie?

    YES

    If the best 5 cards are the same, split the pot.

    Do I have to show my cards when I win?

    NO

    Not if everyone else folded. At a showdown, yes: the last player who bet shows first.

    Is there five of a kind?

    NO

    Not with a normal deck. Only with jokers or wild cards.

    More games

    Try another way to play

    Same hand rankings. Only the dealing changes.

    Five Card Draw

    5 cards each0 shared2 betting rounds

    The classic kitchen-table game, and the easiest to learn.

    1. Everyone puts 1 chip in the pot.
    2. Deal 5 secret cards each. Bet.
    3. Swap: throw away up to 3 cards and get new ones.
    4. Bet again. Then show: best hand wins.

    Omaha

    4 cards each5 shared4 betting rounds

    Played just like Texas Hold'em, but you get 4 cards.

    The catch: use exactly 2 of your cards and exactly 3 shared cards.

    4 hearts on the table plus your A♥ would be a flush in Hold'em. In Omaha it's not: you need 2 hearts in your own hand.

    Seven Card Stud

    7 cards each0 shared5 betting rounds

    No blinds. Some of your cards are face up, so everyone can see part of your hand.

    1. Everyone puts 1 chip in. Deal 2 down, 1 up.
    2. Lowest face-up card starts the betting.
    3. Deal 1 face-up card and bet, three times.
    4. Last card face down. Bet, then show your best 5 of 7.

    Wild cards

    Pick a card that can be anything you want, like all 2s or the jokers. Hands get much bigger, and five of a kind becomes the best hand of all.

    Agree on it before you start dealing.
